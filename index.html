AutoPay SimpleSponsored by CREIGNIFICENT LLC

One link. Monthly autopay.

Send a client one secure link. They choose a bank account or card and authorize recurring billing. Stripe charges them. You watch a simple dashboard.

V1 job
  1. Create the client.
  2. Send the enrollment link.
  3. Client enrolls on Stripe.
  4. Stripe charges every month.
  5. Pause or cancel from the dashboard.

A client only opens their own enrollment link. They cannot see another client. The full list is on the owner dashboard, behind the owner password. No sample clients are shown here.
